Thank you for choosing Beyond Pages. We want every reader to have a smooth experience, and we aim to handle refund requests fairly and respectfully. This Refund Policy explains when a refund may be available and how to ask for one.

1. Digital Products

Our books and e-books are digital products. They are delivered electronically through our Payhip store and are not physical items. Once a digital product has been made available to you for download or access, it cannot be returned in the way a physical product can.

2. When You May Qualify for a Refund

We will consider a refund request in circumstances such as:

• You were charged more than once for the same purchase (a duplicate payment).
• You were charged an incorrect amount.
• A technical problem prevented delivery of your purchase, and we could not reasonably resolve it.
• The file you received is defective or inaccessible, and we could not reasonably provide a working copy.

Each request is considered on its own facts.

3. Purchases That Are Generally Not Refundable

Because digital products can be accessed and copied immediately, refunds may generally not be available where a customer simply changes their mind after downloading or accessing a book. This does not affect any rights you have under applicable consumer protection laws, and we will honour any refund that the law requires.

4. Problems Accessing or Downloading Your Book

If you cannot access, download, or open a book you have purchased, please contact us before requesting a refund. Tell us which device or app you are using and what happens when you try to open the file. This gives us a reasonable opportunity to help, whether by resending your download link, providing the file in another format where one is available, or solving the problem with you directly.

5. Incorrect or Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly, or more than once, please contact us as soon as you notice. Include the email address used for the purchase and the details of the charges so that we can check them against our order records.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy and include:

• The email address used for the purchase.
• Your order details, such as the book title, order number, and date of purchase.
• A brief explanation of the issue, with screenshots if they are helpful.

7. Review and Processing

We review every request fairly and will let you know the outcome. Approved refunds are processed through the payment provider used for your purchase, and are subject to that provider's procedures and timelines. The time it takes for a refund to appear in your account may also depend on your bank or card issuer.

8. Your Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy limits or excludes any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws, including South African consumer protection legislation where it applies to your purchase.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
